**If it is a project**, tell us what the system has to do and what limits it —
budget, timeline, environment, regulatory context. A constraint stated early is
worth more than a specification written late, and rough is fine. If the shape of
the problem is clear we can usually say quickly whether it is something we
should take on.

**If it is not**, that is equally welcome. Research collaborations, joint work
with a lab or another shop, someone building in an adjacent area, or a question
about something on this site — all fair game.
