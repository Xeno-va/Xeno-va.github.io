How the work runs

Find the constraint, not the idea

The search is for one piece of physics that several unrelated buyers are already paying to work around. One constraint with many markets behind it beats one market with many competitors in it.

Build the whole stack

Board, firmware and host software designed together by one person. Interfaces get decided rather than negotiated, and nothing waits on a handoff between teams.

Cost is a design axis

Incumbent instruments routinely cost ten to a hundred times what the job actually requires. Specifying to the job instead of to the category is often the entire product.

Prove it, then claim it

Every build carries the instrument that measures it. A number nothing acts on can be plausible and wrong, and nobody would ever know.

Where the work lands

  • Instruments that have to survive unattended, for months, with nobody visiting
  • Machines whose output is the invoice — where the count has to be believed
  • Autonomy and control on real hardware, with real latency and real backlash
  • Internal tooling for teams who are not programmers and should not have to be

The standing rule

Problem-driven. Evidence over claims. Priced to be bought.

Every decision is written down against the purpose it serves and what it costs — including the ones that turned out wrong. That record is part of the output, not a byproduct of it.
